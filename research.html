Research

Projects, publications, and ongoing work

Current Projects

Coral Proxy Reconstruction

Using stable isotope records (δ18O, δ13C) from coral archives to reconstruct past ocean temperature, salinity, and circulation patterns.

Ongoing

Age Modeling for Coral Archives

Developing robust age models for coral records using X-ray image analysis, peak-matching algorithms, and spectral methods.

Ongoing

Suess Effect Correction

Quantifying and correcting for the Suess effect in coral δ13C records to isolate climate signals from anthropogenic carbon influences.
