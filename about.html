About Me

Geophysicist · Paleoclimatologist · Data Analyst

Background

I am a PhD student in Geophysics at the Jackson School of Geosciences, University of Texas at Austin, where I joined in August 2024. My research focuses on coral-based paleoclimate reconstructions, combining geochemical proxy analysis with time series methods to understand past variability in ocean and climate systems.


I completed my MSc in Physics of Earth and Atmosphere at the Rhenish Friedrich Wilhelm University of Bonn (2020–2023), and my BSc in Geosciences, specialized in Geophysics, also at Bonn (2016–2020). Prior to my PhD, I worked as a Meteorologist, Data Analyst, and Consultant at anemos GmbH in Lüneburg, Germany, focusing on wind atlas and environmental meteorology.


Outside of research, I enjoy water polo, bouldering, playing classical guitar, cinema, literature, and cooking.
